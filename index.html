FORBIDDEN WORDS - 2025

With dozens of words relating to queer, inclusive, anti racist, anti-fascist and intersectional vocabularies being removed from US administrative systems, the importance of language and signs used when it comes to societal dynamics of erasure of marginalized populations and legitimization of systemic persecution is at an all time high as of 2025. This project aims to counter this unprecedented violence and attest that, by denying terminologies and semiotics, the contemporary white binary and heterosexual system imposes an essentialist, reductive and fascist structure on the bodies and identities of marginalized people. Against a white, heteronormative and cisgender ideal, and in perspective with the well problematic theories of capitalist and fascist trans humanism, let's try to prevent the advent of a dystopia that would annihilate the plurality and beauty of human experience, vast and unique in their kind.
